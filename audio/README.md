# Sound effects

Generated by the founder with ElevenLabs and committed to the branch. Every
figure here is MEASURED by decoding the file — length, peak and loudness — not
read off its name.

## What to know before adding more

**Silence is trimmed at runtime, not in these files.** ElevenLabs pads its
output to a round length, and some of these carry up to 374ms of silence BEFORE
the sound and nearly two seconds after. A reel stop is booked to fire at the
exact instant the reel lands, so untrimmed it would arrive a third of a second
late. `sound.ts` cuts both ends on decode, which means uploads can be dropped in
exactly as generated — nothing needs preparing and nothing is re-encoded.

**`peak` above 1.0 means the render clipped.** Usable, slightly crunchy at
volume; adding "clean, undistorted, with headroom, do not clip" to the prompt
usually fixes it.

**Duplicates and duds are listed but unused.** See `api/sound-sets.ts` for what
is actually wired.
